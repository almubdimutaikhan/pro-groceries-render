Same Basket

Brand book v1

Colours, type, corner radii, block sizes and screen templates for the Same Basket web app. Built mobile-first at 375px. It borrows its look from Zimran, Finelo and Coursiv, and adjusts it for US grocery shoppers. Values tagged src in tokens.css were pulled from those sites' live CSS on 14 Nov 2026.

02 · Colour for our audience

The audience is US household shoppers, mostly 28 to 55, budgeting for a family and anxious about the weekly bill. Finelo and Coursiv sit on dark plum, which reads as trading or tech. For groceries we flip to a light, airy ground and keep the family violet for actions. Green is reserved for money the shopper keeps, and yellow for price tags and promos. Every hue already exists in the three sources.

62% ground + white 22% ink 10% violet

08 · Keep it human

Do

  • Use real products, real prices, real store names in every mockup.
  • Show the receipt. It's our most recognisable object; draw it with dashed rules and tabular prices.
  • Write like a friend who's good with money: "Kept yours", "Saves $1.20", "Your call".
  • One highlight word per headline, yellow marker style, like Coursiv.
  • Product photos on plain white tiles (44px thumbs).

Don't

  • No gradients behind text, no glassmorphism, no purple-to-blue hero blobs.
  • No emoji as icons, no sparkle "AI" icons, no robot mascots.
  • No 3-column feature grids with icon + title + sentence.
  • No "Unlock", "Supercharge", "Seamless", "Effortless", "Revolutionize".
  • No fake urgency timers or "only 2 spots left" on the paywall.
